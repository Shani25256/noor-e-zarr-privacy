NOOR-E-ZARR Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 August 2026

Developer: Zeeshan Nazir
Android package: org.zeeshan.noorezarr

NOOR-E-ZARR is a private digital ledger for jewellery and trading businesses.

Data stored

Customer and vendor names, phone numbers, invoices, payments, expenses, inventory and notes are stored in the app's private database on the user's phone.

Cloud backup

Google sign-in is optional. When enabled, the app stores a backup in a file it creates in the user's own Google Drive. The app requests the least-privilege drive.file scope and cannot browse unrelated Drive files. The developer operates no application server and does not receive the ledger.

Security

The optional App Lock PIN is stored as a salted password hash. OAuth tokens remain in private app storage and Android system backup is disabled. Network traffic to Google uses HTTPS.

Exports

Invoices, statements, receipts and backups deliberately exported by the user are placed in Downloads/NOOR-E-ZARR. They remain there until the user deletes them.

Diagnostics and tracking

Technical reports remain on the phone until the user shares them. The app contains no advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking SDK.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until the user deletes the account in Settings, clears app storage or uninstalls the app. Public exports remain in Downloads. Drive backups remain in the user's Drive until the user deletes them.

Open account and data deletion instructions.

Children

This business application is not directed at children.

Contact

Privacy and deletion support is provided through the public NOOR-E-ZARR support tracker. Do not post customer names, phone numbers, invoices, Google tokens or other private business data.
