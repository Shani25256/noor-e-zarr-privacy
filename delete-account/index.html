Delete NOOR-E-ZARR Account and Data

Developer: Shani25256
Android package: org.zeeshan.noorezarr

Delete from the app

  1. Open NOOR-E-ZARR.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account & Data.
  4. Complete both confirmations.

This deletes that account's private ledger, configuration, cached logo and Google token from the phone, then signs out.

Delete remaining copies

  • Delete exported documents from Downloads/NOOR-E-ZARR.
  • Delete the NOOR-E-ZARR backup Sheet from the user's Google Drive.
  • Remove NOOR-E-ZARR access from the Google Account security page.

Request deletion without the app

The developer does not operate a ledger server and therefore does not hold a server-side copy to erase. For deletion assistance or confirmation, submit a request through the link below.

Submit NOOR-E-ZARR deletion request

The request is public. Do not include an email address, customer data, phone number, invoice, token or other private information.
